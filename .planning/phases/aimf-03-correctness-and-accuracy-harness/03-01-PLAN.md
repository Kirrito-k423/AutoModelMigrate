<threat_model>
| Threat | Severity | Mitigation |
|--------|----------|------------|
| T-03-01 Correctness harness treats smoke as complete migration support. | high | Define ordered gates and explicitly separate smoke, parity, accuracy, backend runtime, and optimization readiness. |
| T-03-02 Recipes omit enough environment/baseline detail to reproduce results. | high | Require backend, dtype, hardware, model revision, command, fixture, seed policy, and evidence fields. |
| T-03-03 MiniMax M3 example claims NPU or MSA support before runtime/operator evidence. | high | Mark NPU/MSA gates blocked or unsupported and require root `npu-smi`, CANN, `torch_npu`, tensor smoke, and operator evidence before support claims. |
</threat_model>
